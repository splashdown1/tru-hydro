## What this is

Practical starter ranges and steps for growing plants without soil (roots in nutrient solution or inert media). Focuses on simple home systems that can reach leaf, flower, or fruit. Targets common leafy greens first, then fruiting crops. Values are typical published ranges from extension and grower sources (UF/IFAS-style guidance, university extension summaries, common commercial hydroponic practice). Adjust for your water, climate, variety, and measurements. Always measure; do not guess.

---

## Core numbers (most crops)

| Factor | Typical range | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| **pH** | 5.5–6.5 (target often 5.8–6.2) | Outside this: nutrient lockout (Fe, P, Zn, etc. become unavailable). Check daily or every other day at first. |
| **EC (mS/cm)** | Seedlings 0.4–0.8; leafy greens 1.0–1.8; fruiting (tomato/pepper) 1.8–2.5+ (up to ~3.5 peak) | Start low. Raise gradually. EC rises as water is used; top up with plain water or dilute solution. |
| **Solution temperature** | 18–22 °C (65–72 °F) preferred; keep under ~24 °C | Warmer water holds less oxygen → root problems. |
| **Air / dissolved oxygen** | Continuous aeration in active systems; air gap in passive | Critical for roots. |
| **Photoperiod** | Leafy greens/herbs: 14–16 h light; fruiting often 16–18 h veg then adjust | Consistent timer. |
| **PPFD (µmol/m²/s at canopy)** | Seedlings 100–200; leafy greens ~150–350; fruiting 400–700+ | Measure if possible. Full-spectrum LED preferred. |
| **DLI (mol/m²/day)** | Leafy greens ~12–17; fruiting crops ~20–30 | DLI = PPFD × hours × 0.0036. Higher DLI needed for fruit/flower energy. |

**Water:** use clean source. Test or know baseline EC/hardness. Chlorine/chloramine: let sit or treat if high. Change or refresh reservoir regularly (every 1–3 weeks typical for small systems; watch roots and EC/pH drift).

## Simple systems (start here)

1. **Kratky (passive)** — Best first system for lettuce/herbs. No pump, no electricity.  
   Opaque container (jar, bucket, tote) + lid with net pot hole(s) + inert media (clay pebbles / LECA or rockwool) + nutrient solution. Fill so solution just touches bottom of net pot. As plant drinks, air gap forms for oxygen. Do not refill to the top once roots are established. Limited to smaller/faster crops; reservoir empties by harvest.

2. **DWC (Deep Water Culture)** — Roots hang in aerated solution. Air pump + air stone running continuously. Good for greens and can scale to larger plants. Opaque bucket/tote with lid + net pots. One plant per 5-gallon bucket is common for fruiting types.

3. **Other** — NFT (thin film in channels, continuous pump — good for greens), drip/Dutch bucket (media + drip, better for larger fruiting plants). Start simple; add complexity only after you can hold pH/EC steady.

**Opaque everything that holds solution.** Light on the water grows algae.

## Setup steps (Kratky or basic DWC example)

1. Clean opaque container and lid. Drill/cut holes for net pots (2–3 inch typical).
2. Rinse media (clay pebbles etc.).
3. Start seeds in rockwool/plugs or buy clean seedlings. Transplant when roots show (1–3 weeks typical). Gently rinse soil if starting from soil plants.
4. Mix nutrient solution to target EC (half strength or less for seedlings). Add nutrients to water first, then adjust pH last. Stir well.
5. Place net pot so media/roots just contact solution (Kratky) or are submerged with aeration (DWC).
6. Provide light and airflow. Monitor daily at first.
7. Top up as needed (plain water or dilute solution). Full change when EC drifts high, roots look stressed, or on schedule.

**Materials minimum:** container + lid, net pots, media, hydroponic nutrient (not soil fertilizer), pH meter or kit + up/down solutions, EC/TDS meter (know your scale: often EC × 500 ≈ ppm), thermometer, timer + lights if indoor, air pump/stone for DWC.

## Light

- Full-spectrum LED preferred (efficient, less heat).
- Distance: follow fixture guidance; typically 12–24 inches, closer for lower-power, farther for high-intensity to avoid burn.
- Leafy: 14–16 h is common.
- Fruiting (tomato, pepper, strawberry, etc.): higher intensity and often longer photoperiod in vegetative phase; many move toward higher red spectrum emphasis or adjust hours for flower/fruit set. Indoor fruiting usually needs hand pollination (vibrate/tap flowers) and support/pruning.
- Measure PPFD at canopy if you can. Over-lighting leafy greens can cause tip burn; under-lighting fruiting crops yields poor set and small fruit.

## Water, nutrients, stages

- **Order:** mix nutrients → measure EC → adjust pH.
- Leafy greens stay on a more vegetative (higher N) profile.
- Fruiting crops: raise overall EC and shift toward higher P/K (bloom formula) once flowering starts. Calcium important (blossom-end rot risk if low or uneven uptake).
- **Stages rough guide:**
  - Germination / seedling: low EC, high humidity, modest light.
  - Vegetative: increase light and EC, strong roots and leaf.
  - Flower / fruit: higher light demand, adjusted nutrient ratio, support, pollination, watch for deficiencies (Ca, Mg, K common).
- Harvest leafy in 3–8 weeks typical. Fruiting (cherry tomato example) often 75–110+ days to first harvest depending on variety and conditions.

## Getting to fruit or flower

- Choose compact/dwarf varieties for small systems.
- Strong continuous light (higher PPFD/DLI).
- Stable root-zone oxygen, temperature, pH, EC.
- Transition nutrient profile at first flower.
- Air movement, support structures, and (indoors) manual pollination.
- Prune suckers on indeterminate tomatoes if desired; keep leaves from touching lights.
- Watch for stress signals: yellowing, tip burn, stalled growth, root browning/slime (oxygen or pathogen issue).

## Common failure modes (and limits)

- pH drift outside 5.5–6.5 → lockout. Measure and correct small amounts.
- High reservoir temperature → low oxygen → root rot.
- Light leaks into solution → algae.
- Overfeeding (high EC) → tip burn / salt stress. Underfeeding → pale slow growth.
- Pump/air failure in active systems → rapid root death.
- Inconsistent light or temperature → bolting (greens) or poor fruit set.
- Hard or unknown source water without testing can fight your pH/EC targets.
